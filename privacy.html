2. General Information on Data Processing

The "StashSnap" app itself does not collect, process, or store any personal data. There is no transfer of data to our own servers or analysis by us as the provider. If personal data is processed (e.g., when using iCloud or speech recognition), this is done exclusively by Apple within the scope of their services.

3. Use of Apple Services

a) iCloud / CloudKit

If you use the iCloud functionality of the app, your data (e.g., photos, stash information) is exclusively stored in your personal iCloud account. The storage and processing of this data is carried out by Apple within the scope of your iCloud account. As the app provider, we do not have access to this data.

Further information on data processing by Apple can be found in Apple's Privacy Policy:https://www.apple.com/de/legal/privacy/

b) Speech Recognition

If you use the speech recognition function of the app, your voice data is sent to Apple for processing. The conversion of speech to text is done exclusively by the services provided by Apple. As the app provider, we do not receive access to your voice data.

The use of this function requires corresponding consent within the iOS system permissions.

Further information on speech recognition and data privacy at Apple:https://support.apple.com/de-de/HT210673

4. No Disclosure to Third Parties

We, as the app provider, do not share your data with third parties.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, you can assert your data protection rights (e.g., access, deletion, rectification) directly with Apple, provided data is processed via iCloud or other Apple services.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be adjusted occasionally to comply with current legal requirements or to implement changes in the app. The current version can always be found in the app.
