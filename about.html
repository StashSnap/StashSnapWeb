About StashSnap

StashSnap is an iOS app designed to help you organize, catalog, and keep track of your collections, storage spaces, or any group of items. Whether you want to manage your garage, attic, hobby supplies, or any stash of items, StashSnap makes it easy and visual.

  • Photo-based Cataloging: Take a photo of your stash and visually mark each item directly on the image.
  • Automatic Item Detection: Uses on-device AI to segment and detect items in your photo for quick labeling.
  • Speech Recognition: Name items by voice for fast, hands-free cataloging.
  • iCloud Sync: All your data is securely stored in your personal iCloud account, so you can access your stashes across devices.
  • Search & Siri Integration: Find your items quickly with powerful search and Siri voice commands.
  • Privacy-first: No data is sent to our servers. All processing happens on your device or via Apple services.

StashSnap is perfect for anyone who wants to keep track of what they own, where it's stored, and find it again easily. The companion web app lets you view and manage your stashes from any browser, synced via iCloud.
