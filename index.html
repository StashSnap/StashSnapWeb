Why Choose StashSnap?

Photo-Based Cataloging

Take photos of your storage spaces and visually mark each item directly on the image for intuitive organization.

AI-Powered Detection

Uses on-device AI to automatically segment and detect items in your photos for quick and accurate labeling.

Voice Recognition

Name items by voice for fast, hands-free cataloging. Perfect for when you're busy organizing.

iCloud Sync

All your data is securely stored in your personal iCloud account, accessible across all your devices.

Smart Search

Find your items quickly with powerful search and Siri voice commands. Never lose track of your belongings again.

Privacy-First

No data is sent to our servers. All processing happens on your device or via Apple services for maximum privacy.
